Table format & sign convention

Use a complete rectangular grid in Re and signed spin ratio S = ωD/(2V). Columns Re,S,CD,CM; C_D and C_M also accepted. Bilinear interpolation; values outside the grid use the nearest boundary, with a warning.

CM is signed along +vertical × ball velocity. Positive CM bends left. This differs from some Fluent inlet-flow conventions: check and, if needed, reverse the sign. Zero spin should have CM = 0 for a pure Magnus model.

The CSV from the earlier sweep script is accepted directly: if U_m_s and omega_rad_s columns are present, interpolation uses that original rectangular velocity × spin grid. Keep the same diameter and fluid properties as your Fluent study.

Model, equations & sources

m dv/dt = Fᴅ + Fᴍ + mg. Drag opposes velocity. Sidespin is vertical and constant; Magnus force is horizontal, perpendicular to horizontal velocity. Still air. Fourth-order Runge–Kutta, Δt = 0.003 s.

Ball: D = 0.229 m, m = 0.43 kg. Air: ρ = 1.225 kg/m³, μ = 1.7894×10⁻⁵ Pa·s. Gravity: 9.81 m/s². Area A = πD²/4.

Approximation: CD = 0.275; CM = sign(RPM) × 0.25[1 − exp(−|RPM|/300)]. These are constant during each flight. The 300 RPM scale is an illustrative choice, not a fitted correlation. Bray & Kerwin (2003) report CD ≈ 0.25–0.30 and lift coefficients ≈ 0.23–0.29. Linthorne & Everett (2011) discuss a saturating spin–lift relationship.

Fᴅ = −½ρAV²CD v/V. Fᴍ = ½ρAV²CM (e_z × vₕ)/|vₕ|. Re = ρVD/μ. S = ωD/(2V). Imported CM uses the same fixed positive-spin direction. Forces and speed update as the ball slows.

Wall collision uses a solid rectangular envelope; displayed players are illustrative. Frame collisions are conservative box tests, including ball radius. The whole ball must clear the goal line inside the frame to score. Ground contact ends flight; no bouncing, goalkeeper, wall jumping, or spin decay.
